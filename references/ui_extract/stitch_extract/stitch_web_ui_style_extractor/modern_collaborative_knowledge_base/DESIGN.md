## Brand & Style

This design system channels an intentional, calm, and highly focused workspace aesthetic. It sits at the intersection of Scandinavian minimalism, warm editorial publishing, and contemporary SaaS productivity. The design eliminates unnecessary noise to amplify clarity, structured thinking, and effortless team collaboration.

The target audience comprises modern distributed teams, knowledge workers, creators, and product-led organizations who value focus, high-fidelity writing environments, and seamless information discovery. 

The emotional tone evokes clarity, focus, warmth, and effortless capability. Interfaces feel tactile yet featherlight, combining paper-like warm neutrals (`#FDF9F4`) with a sharp, decisive ink neutral (`#18191B`) and a vibrant, warm vermilion accent (`#F46A38`) to direct attention without overwhelming the user's focus.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an organic, paper-inspired reading foundation contrasted against high-precision digital accents:

- **Primary (`#F46A38`):** An energetic vermilion orange used strategically for primary call-to-actions, live cursor indicators, active collaborative states, and key highlights. It provides immediate contrast against warm neutral backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#4B8BE8`):** A soft cerulean blue reserved for informational callouts, link anchors, collaborative presence avatars, and multi-user cursor tracking.
- **Tertiary (`#F8EFE4`):** A tinted almond sand that forms secondary navigation rails, subtle table headers, code snippet backdrops, and active row indicators.
- **Neutral (`#18191B`):** An off-black charcoal that grounds all high-hierarchy typography and primary structural elements, eliminating the harshness of pure black while retaining optimal contrast ratios.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDF9F4`):** The primary paper-white background that softens eye strain during prolonged writing and reading sessions.
- **Subtle Border (`#EBE3D7`):** An organic low-contrast divider token that provides structure without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headlines and titles with **Inter** for sustained document reading, data density, and interface controls. 

- **Display & Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers contemporary geometric balance with soft, humanized terminals, echoing modern editorial polish without feeling corporate.
- **Document & Interface Body:** Inter provides neutral, highly legible micro-details, consistent x-heights, and robust tabular alignment across nested documents and navigation trees.
- **Reading Rhythm:** In document bodies (`body-lg`), line heights are intentionally generous (`28px` on `18px` font size) to foster fatigue-free continuous reading. Letter spacing is subtly pulled tighter (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on display tiers to retain visual cohesion at large scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts a dynamic hybrid grid balancing navigation sidebars with an author-centric document canvas:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Three-tier layout composed of a collapsible navigation rail (240px - 280px), an optional contextual channel/list view (320px), and a centered document reading canvas constrained to a maximum width of `760px` for optimal typographic measure (60-75 characters per line). Section margins sit at `2.5rem` (`space-xl`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Sidebars collapse into overlay slide-overs or a floating rail icon bar. The document canvas expands to fill available width with standard `1.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked layout with edge margins of `1rem` (`margin-mobile`). Toolbars transition to sticky bottom action bars, and inline document headers reflow cleanly without clipping.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Base-8 spacing scale with granular 4px increments for tightly bound icon-label pairings (`space-xs`) and compact list nodes (`space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy artificial drop shadows in favor of **tonal layering**, **whisper-soft borders**, and **subtle ambient diffusion**:

- **Ground (Canvas):** Surfaces render in `#FDF9F4` as the base canvas.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** Lifted content cards use crisp `#FFFFFF` backgrounds bound by a 1px solid border in `#EBE3D7`. No drop shadow is applied in rest states.
- **Surface Level 2 (Flyouts & Dropdowns):** Floating menus, slash commands, and search bars utilize a faint ambient tint: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 25, 27, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 25, 27, 0.02)` bordered by `#EBE3D7`.
- **Modals & Command Center:** Overlaid modals maintain a centered, elevated posture with `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(24, 25, 27, 0.08)` paired with an ultra-soft backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background-color: rgba(24, 25, 27, 0.15)`).
- **Interactive Tonal Shift:** Interactive list items and rows elevate via background color shift to `#F8EFE4` upon hover rather than utilizing physical translation or shadow increases.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined `roundedness: 2` (base 0.5rem / 8px), projecting friendliness while retaining structural discipline:

- **Base Controls:** Form inputs, buttons, search triggers, and inline badges adopt `rounded-md` (`0.5rem / 8px`).
- **Cards & Document Blocks:** Callout blocks, embedded cards, and interactive containers use `rounded-lg` (`1rem / 16px`).
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Floating command bars, onboarding drawers, and global search modals use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem / 24px`).
- **Presence & Avatars:** Fully circular pills (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for team member avatars, status indicators, and pill tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#F46A38` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius. Padding: `0.5rem 1rem`. Micro-interaction: subtle brightness step down on active (`#E05826`), no heavy shadow.
- **Secondary / Neutral:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #EBE3D7` border and `#18191B` text. On hover, background transitions smoothly to `#F8EFE4`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#18191B` or `#6E6F73` text; active hover fills with `#F8EFE4`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background defaults to `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#EBE3D7` stroke and `0.5rem` corner radius. 
- Focus state switches the border to `#F46A38` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(244, 106, 56, 0.15)`).
- Global command/quick-search bar sits flat with an inline keyboard shortcut chip (`⌘K`) in `#F8EFE4`.

### Document Tree & Navigation Lists
- Ultra-clean row items with 4px vertical padding and 8px horizontal padding.
- Selected state adopts a solid `#F8EFE4` fill with medium weight text (`#18191B`) and a 3px accent vertical bar in `#F46A38` anchored to the left border.
- Hover states initiate a fast 120ms fade to `#FDF9F4`.

### Cards & Doc Surfaces
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `#EBE3D7` borders and 16px internal padding.
- Document templates and wiki cards feature an upper thumbnail block with a warm neutral accent strip, title in `headline-sm`, and collaborator stacked avatars on the bottom right.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped tags with `0.25rem 0.625rem` padding, utilizing low-saturation background tints (e.g., `#F8EFE4` for general labels, faint `#4B8BE8` 10% opacity for informational metadata). Text in `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Form Toggles
- Custom square with `4px` radius. Rest state uses 1.5px border in `#EBE3D7`. Checked state triggers a quick scale-in transition filled with `#F46A38` displaying a white checkmark.

### Collaborative Callouts & Slash Command Menus
- **Callout Blocks:** Full-width rounded panels (`0.5rem`) with 12px inset padding, light `#F8EFE4` background, and left indicator accent line matching contextual tone (e.g., `#4B8BE8` for notes, `#F46A38` for critical tasks).
- **Slash Menu:** Floating dropdown layered at Surface Level 2 with keyboard navigation highlighting items using `#F8EFE4` and displaying inline JetBrains Mono shortcut tokens.
